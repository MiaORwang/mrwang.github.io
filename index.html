01 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity, science,
real-world impact.

I am an interdisciplinary agricultural and environmental scientist with a background in soil physics, wetland research, crop health, spatial analysis and environmental assessment. I earned my PhD in 2024 through the DFG Research Training Group Baltic TRANSCOAST at the University of Rostock, Germany, studying the physical and hydraulic properties of peatland soils and their implications for rewetting and restoration.

My research has combined field and laboratory experiments, spatial sampling, GIS and statistical modelling to investigate soil, water and nutrient dynamics. This foundation continues to inform my interest in environmental heterogeneity, land-use change and sustainable management.

Today, my work is increasingly centered on agricultural process engineering and data-driven livestock research, including sensor-based monitoring, animal welfare assessment, spatial analysis and AI-assisted methods. I am particularly interested in turning reliable measurements into practical decision support for precision agriculture and smart livestock farming.
