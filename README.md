# Miaorun Wang | Research Portfolio

**Agricultural & Environmental Scientist · Dr. agr. · University of Rostock, Germany**

Welcome to my research portfolio. My work connects **agriculture, animal welfare, environmental science, and data-driven methods** to better understand biological and agricultural systems and support evidence-based decision-making.

🌐 **[Explore my research portfolio](https://miaorwang.github.io/mrwang.github.io/)**

## About me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher at the **University of Rostock**. My current work focuses on digital livestock farming, sensor-based monitoring, animal behavior, and the analysis of agricultural data. My academic background also includes soil physics, peatland hydrology, wetland ecosystems, and crop production.

## Research interests

- **Smart livestock & animal welfare:** Sensor-based monitoring and animal behavior assessment.
- **Precision agriculture & sensing:** Digital technologies and multisensor approaches for agricultural applications.
- **Agricultural & environmental data science:** Statistical analysis, modelling, and spatial data visualization.
- **Soil and wetland systems:** Peatland hydro-physical properties, nutrient dynamics, and ecosystem restoration.

## Publications

My research includes peer-reviewed publications on peatland and soil hydro-physical properties, wetland nutrient dynamics, coastal groundwater discharge, and crop protection.
